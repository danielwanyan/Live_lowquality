Live Low Quality Rules v1

Task
Review 5 live slices and detect exactly 19 live-room low-quality categories. Check every category independently. Do not omit, merge, rename, skip, or reorder any category. Use original foreign-language ASR/OCR semantics. Final human-readable evidence should be Chinese; direct source quotes may remain in the original language.

Materials
Common materials for all categories: Slice images, ASR, OCR.
Product images may be used only for categories 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17, 19. Product images are forbidden for the other 9 categories.

Global evidence rules
Every hit requires concrete evidence from allowed materials.
Evidence must identify material type and slice number.
Do not infer beyond listed definitions, thresholds, and exemptions.
If evidence is weak or threshold is not met, output no_hit for that category.

Exclusive priority rules
Category 3 has absolute priority over category 6. If there is no clear sustained product promotion, explanation, or demonstration intent and live content is unrelated to bound products, classify as category 3 and do not classify as category 6.
Category 1 has priority over category 19. Sexual exposure or sexualized implication belongs to category 1, not category 19.

Category 1: 色情低俗低质问题 / sexual_vulgar
Allowed materials: slice_image, ASR. Product image forbidden.
Hit when any one of 5 slice images or 5 ASR snippets shows one of the listed sexual-vulgar scenarios: underwear with visible private-part protrusion, close-up thigh-root exposure, padded/fake body-shape exaggeration, more than 50% buttocks exposure in tight underwear, sexualized thigh exposure, underwear/bikini with hip-swinging, explicit sexual teasing such as chest/butt shaking or deliberate cleavage exposure by bending, clothing/product exposing large skin area judged unsuitable for public display, or product/clothing likely to expose sexual-organ outline. ASR hit only when sexual terms are frequently used during product promotion.
Exempt: ordinary cleavage exposure without deliberate sexualized action; normal bikini in beach context.

Category 2: 恶心恐怖低质问题 / disgusting_horror
Allowed materials: slice_image, ASR. Product image forbidden.
Visual hit requires both: indoor scene with disgusting/horror content over 50% of frame or many insects clearly spread through environment, and no active cleaning. ASR hit requires mentioning such disgusting/horror scene and no cleaning. Any active cleaning behavior or cleaning product use exempts.
Threshold: any one image or ASR hit.

Category 3: 商品无关低质问题 / product_unrelated
Allowed materials: slice_image, ASR, OCR, product_image. Product image allowed.
Use bound product image category/function/use-case as the only product-reference standard.
Hit if live core content is unrelated to bound product and lacks valid sustained product promotion.
Threshold: at least 3 ASR snippets fully unrelated to bound products; or at least 3 frame groups show no clear bound product and matching ASR is unrelated; or unrelated image+ASR count totals at least 6.
No exemption once threshold is met.

Category 4: 无互动低质问题 / no_interaction
Allowed materials: slice_image, ASR, OCR. Product image forbidden.
Single snippet hit requires both no product promotion and no host-audience interaction.
Threshold: at least 4 of 5 ASR snippets hit.

Category 5: 无实物商品展示低质问题 / no_physical_product_display
Allowed materials: slice_image, ASR, OCR, product_image. Product image allowed.
Hit when live does not show bound physical product as real video: product only as sticker/cutout/photo/screenshot/static image; no bound product shown; or only unrecognizable product part shown. ASR/OCR are supporting evidence only when relevant.
Threshold: at least 4 of 5 frame groups hit.

Category 6: 商品描述不详细低质问题 / product_description_not_detailed
Allowed materials: slice_image, ASR, OCR, product_image. Product image allowed.
Prerequisite: clear product promotion intent and sustained product display/explanation/demonstration.
Hit if promotion gives no effective product information such as category/spec/function/use/price/discount, or product talk is mostly off-topic with very low effective information, or product is shown/demonstrated with no useful explanation.
Exempt: merely wearing/showing product with no promotion/explanation. If no promotion intent and content is unrelated, use category 3 instead.
Threshold: cumulative at least 4 image/ASR evidence units.

Category 7: 静态帧遮挡低质问题 / static_frame_occlusion
Allowed materials: slice_image. Product image forbidden.
Hit when any frame has an occlusion/interruption layer covering at least 80% of live screen and seriously blocking the underlying live content.
Both 80%+ coverage and serious viewing interference are required.

Category 8: 非母语违规低质问题 / non_native_language_violation

Category 12: 功能效果虚假宣传低质问题 / false_function_effect_claim
